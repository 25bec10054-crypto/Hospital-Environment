Accessible Wearable Engineering

While smartwatches often cost ₹15,000–₹40,000, our concept prototype is built using commodity open-source embedded electronics designed for under ₹1,200.

  • ⚡
    ESP32 Microcontroller

    Low-power dual-core processor with integrated Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and Wi-Fi.

  • 💡
    MAX30102 High-Sensitivity Pulse Oximeter

    Red and Infrared LEDs with photodetector to measure reflected PPG light curves.

  • 🛡️
    False-Alarm Protected SOS

    A single press triggers a 15-second phone countdown with Cancel button to eliminate accidental alarms.

Academic & Clinical Disclaimer: Readings are experimental and designed for wellness/research demonstration, not clinical diagnosis. Blood pressure is not measured.
Emergency Response

The 5-Step SOS Safety Protocol

How SwasthyaLink balances rapid emergency dispatch with strict false-alarm safeguards.

1

Trigger SOS

Tap the in-app emergency button or hold the wearable band button.

2

15s Safety Countdown

Configurable timer with prominent "Cancel – I'm OK" option for accidental activation.

3

Automatic Decision

If confirmed, alert is sent. If user does not respond before timeout, escalated as UNCONFIRMED EMERGENCY.

4

GPS & Contacts Alerted

Coordinates and emergency contact notifications dispatched in real time.

5

Provider Triage

Hospital emergency command center acknowledges and dispatches assistance.

Academic Research Hub

Smart Hospital Management System Specifications

Structured academic prototype specifications: Architecture, Role-Based Access, and 5-Member Engineering division.

4-Layer System Architecture

1. Presentation Layer (Role-Based GUI)
Java Swing desktop interfaces & responsive web dashboards tailored to each role.
2. Application Layer (Validation & Rules)
Double-booking prevention, token state machines, triage escalation algorithms.
3. Connectivity Layer (JDBC Driver)
Parameterized SQL queries, secure connection pooling, transaction isolation.
4. Data Layer (Relational MySQL Database)
Structured entities: Users, Patients, Doctors, Appointments, EHR, Prescriptions, Bills, SOS.

5-Member Engineering Team Work Division

Member Assigned Modules & Responsibilities
Member 1 Patient registration, profile management, medical records, and patient portal view.
Member 2 Doctor directory, department mapping, speciality-based discovery, and appointment booking.
Member 3 Token queue state machine, check-in, consultation notes, and e-prescription builder.
Member 4 SOS workflow, emergency contacts, GPS geocoding demonstration, and hospital finder.
Member 5 MySQL schema design, authentication & RBAC, pharmacy & billing integration, shared testing.
